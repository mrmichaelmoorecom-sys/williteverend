how is this computed?

"Chance it actually ends" = 1 − P(stays past Jan 20, 2029). P(stays) = P(Trump wins the 2028 election) + P(the 2028 election does not occur), treated as disjoint, using the mean of Kalshi and Polymarket where both price the same question (Kalshi alone for the election-occurs leg). "Chance it ends early" is the highest-priced of Kalshi's nested early-exit markets (leaves office / resigns / impeached-and-removed / 25th Amendment) — the maximum, not the sum, because they overlap. Prices are order-book midpoints; thin markets (wide spread or low liquidity) are ignored when a solid one exists. No market on either venue prices the president's death; Kalshi's "leaves office" market explicitly excludes it. Approval does not enter the formula. The answer flips to YES when the term-end date passes, when a Kalshi exit market settles YES, when a Polymarket exit market resolves YES, or by a manual override.
